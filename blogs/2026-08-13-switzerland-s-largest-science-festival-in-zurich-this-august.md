It all kicks off on 22 August when over 1,000 researchers from ETH Zurich and the University of Zurich present their work at exhibition booths and in labs, lecture halls and shows. Start-ups will present their future projects, and the City of Zurich’s municipal enterprises will open their doors for an exclusive look behind the scenes.
